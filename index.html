Month-by-month table

How this is worked out: each month uses the total you set aside on both pay days. Minimums are paid first, then extra goes to the card you chose to pay off first. Unpaid balances grow by the card's yearly interest divided by 12. Future minimums keep the same share of the balance as this month's, plus installments, and never fall below 5% of this month's amount due. No new purchases are added. This is an estimate, not your bank's figures.
